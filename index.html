About Me

Health Geographer, Geospatial Data Scientist, and Drone Remote Sensing Specialist

Peter Haoxuan Ge is a health geographer and geospatial data scientist. He is currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Geography and Planning at the University of Toronto and the Department of Geography, Geomatics and Environment (GGE) at the University of Toronto Mississauga (UTM).

He is affiliated with the GISPark Lab and RSSEM Lab, under the supervision of Dr. Jue Wang and Dr. Yuhong He, respectively. His work connects health geography, time geography, GIScience, spatial statistics, and drone remote sensing.

Peter’s research focuses on how environmental exposures, human mobility, physical activity, and daily space-time contexts shape health and well-being. He applies GPS trajectory data, wearable sensors, geospatial big data, and spatiotemporal analysis to examine relationships among built environments, physical activity, psychological stress, and type-2 diabetes-related outcomes.

In addition to his research, Peter teaches and develops course materials in GIS and drone remote sensing. He works as a GIS Specialist at the Ontario Heritage Trust. Outside academia, he enjoys photography, travelling by public transportation, and playing the keyboard synthesizer.
